Privacy Policy

App Name: PDF Image Tools
Package ID: com.aistudio.pdfimagetools.kxmpqr
Effective Date: 2026-11-13

PDF Image Tools is built with privacy first. All PDF and image processing features in this app run 100% locally and offline on your device. We do not operate a backend server to upload, store, analyze, or share your documents or photos.

1. Files, Photos & Documents

When you select images or PDF files using the system Photo Picker or File Picker, or capture a photo via your device camera app, processing happens strictly in local memory and temporary app storage on your device. Files are only saved to your chosen location or shared when you explicitly tap Save or Share.

2. Local Preferences & Recent Files

The app stores basic preferences (selected language, Light/Dark/System theme mode, recent output file metadata such as file name/size/timestamp, and ad-free pass timers) locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. Clearing app data or tapping "Clear" removes this local history.

3. Permissions

The app follows least-privilege practices and does not request broad storage access or internet permissions for core offline tools. Media selection uses Android's zero-permission system Photo Picker and Document Picker.

4. External Links

Links inside Settings (such as Rate Us, Share App, and Contact Support) open Google Play Store, your device share sheet, or your email app only when you tap them. The app does not run background network tracking or upload any files.

5. Children's Privacy

This utility app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
